#### C. Trending Techniques/Routines Scrunch Could Feature

1. **Gel Cast Method (SOTC)** — Apply gel → dry to a "cast" → scrunch out the crunch. THE core CGM technique. Scrunch should have a dedicated tutorial flow for this.
2. **Bowl Method** — Dip hair in bowl of water after product application for maximum clump formation. Viral for wavy types.
3. **Hair Cycling** — Rotating between clarifying, moisturizing, and protein treatments on different wash days. TikTok-born concept gaining mainstream traction.
4. **Squish to Condish (S2C)** — Scrunching conditioner into hair with water to encourage curl clumps. Foundational CGM.
5. **Microplopping** — Using microfiber towel sections to individually plop curl clumps. More precise than standard plopping.
6. **Scalp-First Routines** — Scalp exfoliation, serums, and massage before cleansing. Huge trend crossing into curly space.
7. **Heatless Curls/Sock Curls** — Overnight styling with socks/rollers. Massive for wavy types wanting definition without damage.
8. **Root Clipping** — Clips at roots during drying for volume/lift. Simple technique with viral results.
9. **Refresh Routines** — Misting + scrunching + diffusing between wash days. Critical for multi-day curl maintenance.
10. **Abbey Yung's 11-Step Method** — The maximalist viral routine (detangle → pre-wash oil → scalp exfoliate → shampoo → mask → conditioner → leave-in → style → dry → finish). Scrunch could offer a "simplified" version.
