## Task: Legal & Attribution Analysis for Influencer Content Integration

### Scope
Assess legal risks and implementation approach for integrating #HairTok content (influencer routines, product mentions) into Scrunch. Define safe practices for attribution, media usage, and potential false-endorsement claims.

### Outcome
SUCCESS — Green light with clear guardrails

### Key Decisions

✅ **SAFE:**
- Product names/data in catalog (factual, not copyrightable; descriptive fair use)
- Routine descriptions (functional instructions, not creative works)
- Influencer attribution (proper credit mitigates all risk)
- Links to TikTok videos (drives traffic, creator-approved practice)

❌ **HIGH RISK:**
- Video/image reproduction (copyrighted; requires written permission)
- Implied endorsement ("She recommends Scrunch" — false advertising)
- Copy-pasted captions (derivative work; paraphrase instead)

**Phase 1 (MVP): Week 1 — 1-2 engineer-days**
- TrendingRoutines.tsx component
- Abbey Yung's 5-step routine (manually transcribed, linked)
- Add "Trending on TikTok" section to navigation

**Phase 2 (Scale): Week 2-3 — 2-3 engineer-days**

- Admin UI (CRUD for Shari)
- "As Seen On" badge on product cards
- Support 5–10 routines

**Phase 3 (Community): Month 2+ — Optional 3-5 days**
- User submissions + moderation queue
- Contributor attribution

### Attribution Checklist

- [ ] Creator name + username visible on every routine
- [ ] Link to original TikTok (working, tested)
- [ ] Steps paraphrased in own words (not copy-pasted captions)
- [ ] No video embeds, screenshots, or images without permission
- [ ] "As featured in" positioning (not "endorsed by")
- [ ] Product links accurate and verified
