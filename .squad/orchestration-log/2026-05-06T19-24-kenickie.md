## Work Done

Kenickie analyzed Scrunch's adoption barriers and messaging gaps. Identified three critical concerns blocking first-time user conversion:

1. **"What is this?" problem (CRITICAL)** — Homepage doesn't explain *why* Scrunch exists or how it helps. New visitors see "Browse Products" and bounce because they don't understand the value or differentiation vs. Reddit, TikTok, CurlScan, IsItCG.

2. **Value Prop Not Differentiated (HIGH)** — Scrunch's unique strengths (personalized recommendations, all-in-one UX, ad-free, built by someone with curly hair) are invisible on the homepage. Generic positioning ("Find your perfect products") sounds like every competitor.

3. **First-Visit Path Unclear (HIGH)** — New visitor lands, sees 400+ products or trending #HairTok, has no clear next action. No onboarding flow sets expectation that signup leads to personalized value.

## Key Recommendations

**Mission Statement (1-sentence):**  
"Give curly and wavy hair people the personalized product guidance and community wisdom they need to find products that actually work for *their* hair."

**Proposed Messaging:**  
"Get personalized product recommendations based on your hair, not just trends."

**Three Quick Wins:**
1. Add sub-heading to hero explaining differentiator (1 hour, high impact)
2. Change CTA "Browse Products" → "Create Your Hair Profile" (5 min, high impact)
3. Add "How It Works" 3-step visual below hero (2 hours, high impact)
